Cleans up YouTube on your phone and on desktop. Built for iOS, where you can't change the app.

## What it does

- Hides the Shorts button and Shorts rows
- Hides community posts and the promo banners at the top of Home
- Swaps holiday logos back to the normal logo
- Lets you hide and reorder sidebar items, and adds Subscriptions, Watch later and Playlists
- Can hide the recommended videos on Home, or the whole feed

These three are off until you turn them on:

- **Keep playing in background tabs.** Switching tabs no longer pauses the video.
- **Picture in Picture button.** Tap it before leaving the app and the audio keeps going.
- **Stop video previews.** Thumbnails stop playing previews while you scroll.

## Settings

Open the sidebar and tap the gear at the bottom. Settings can be backed up and restored from there.

## If something breaks

If the sidebar buttons ever disappear, add one of these to the end of the YouTube URL and reload:

- `#ytdc-reset` clears all settings
- `#ytdc-show` unhides every sidebar item
- `#ytdc-safe` turns the sidebar features off for this visit

## On iPhone

Tested with Stay in Edge. Two things iOS won't allow, so don't expect them:

- Audio carrying on by itself after you leave the app. Use the PiP button, or press play on the lock screen.
- Subtitles inside the PiP window.
